## 1. What this is about

Research project: "LLMs as proxy survey participants". You donate chat exports from Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp and answer Likert-style surveys. A local LLM uses your chats (retrieval, **not** training) to imitate how you answer such surveys. Your proxy is compared against your real survey answers. Thesis research only.

## 2. What we collect

- **Chat export files** you choose to upload (Facebook/Instagram JSON, WhatsApp txt), including media (images, voice messages) at low export quality. You decide which conversations to include — exclude anything you don't want processed. Media is stored and protected under the same safeguards as the text (§6).
- **Survey responses.**
- **Contact email** for project communication.

Your chats unavoidably contain data about your **conversation partners** and **persons mentioned** in them. We process that as an integral part of the corpus.

## 3. Purpose & use

- Research: building your personal retrieval corpus so the LLM can imitate **you**.
- **Never**: raw corpus publication, commercial use, demos, startup/portfolio use, model training.
- Conversation partners are **not** analyzed, quoted, profiled, or reported individually. Messages serve only as retrieval context for imitating you.

## 4. Legal basis

**(a) You (donor):** processing of your personal data is based on your **consent** — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; special categories (private messages, survey answers) on your **explicit consent** — Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. Consent is freely given: no compensation, no academic or other consequences for refusing or withdrawing.

**(b) Other persons in your chats** (conversation partners + mentioned persons): **not covered by your consent** — you cannot consent on their behalf, and we don't ask you to. Their data is processed under the **university research regime**: Art. 6(1)(e) (public-interest research) and Art. 9(2)(j) GDPR + §10 databeskyttelsesloven (research processing of special-category data — consent not required by law).

Individual notice to each of them is **exempt under Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR**: we have no channel to reach them (platforms expose no API; you are the only contact), individual outreach would be disproportionate to the research value, and pre-notification would seriously impair the research (it breaks the corpus↔survey timing and biases the sample). **Substitute transparency:** this document, the public project page, and the university research-registry entry describe the processing; they retain full rights anyway — §8.

## 5. Telling your conversation partners — voluntary, never required

You **may** tell your contacts about the project; you are **not required** to, and participation is not conditioned on it. If you have informed some contacts, you may mention it to us (logged as supplementary notice, Art. 14(5)(a)) — helpful but optional.

## 6. Storage, security, access

- **Processor:** uCloud (DeiC, Denmark) under a data-processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). **No transfer outside the EU/EEA.**
- **Safeguards** (Art. 89(1), 32, 25 GDPR): names replaced by SUBJECT/OTHER labels in the working corpus; pseudonymized storage; encryption (in transit + at rest); access limited to the 3 named team members (2 students) + 2 supervisors.
- The project is covered by a **data-protection impact assessment (DPIA, Art. 35)** reviewed with the DPO (Art. 35(2)) and an internal record (Art. 30).

## 7. Retention & destruction

Your corpus is kept until **[12 months after thesis assessment — exact date per DPIA]**, then destroyed including backups, with written confirmation on request. Only **non-identifiable aggregate results** (e.g. group-level statistics) remain in the thesis and academic dissemination. Early erasure available anytime — §9.

## 8. Your rights — and theirs

**You (donor):** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection; **data portability** in machine-readable format for your survey responses (not the chat corpus — it contains third-party data, and porting it would adversely affect others' rights, Art. 20(4)); **withdraw consent anytime** with effect from that point; complain to Datatilsynet.

**Conversation partners & mentioned persons** (no account, no login, no notice channel): an **opt-out mailbox** — [email]. They identify themselves and the relevant corpus; we locate their data via donor-side key, delete it, and confirm in writing. No profiling and no automated decisions affecting them (Art. 22 GDPR).

**Minors:** if chats contain data about minors, the same safeguards apply. As a donation policy, you must be **18+** to donate.

## 9. Withdrawal & objections — procedure

- **You:** email [project mailbox] with "withdraw consent" → erasure of your corpus within 30 days → written confirmation.
- **Other person:** opt-out mailbox [email] → same procedure (objection, Art. 21(6) GDPR → erasure, Art. 17).
